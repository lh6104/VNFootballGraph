================================================================================
⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES
================================================================================

1. REGEX PATTERNS ARE POWERFUL
   - Pattern r"sinh năm \d{4}" catches "sinh năm 1997", "sinh năm 2001", etc.
   - This is MUCH better than just keyword "sinh năm"
   - Patterns detect context, not just words

2. VIETNAMESE + ENGLISH = COMPLETE COVERAGE
   - Vietnamese keywords: Vietnamese players
   - English keywords: Foreign coaches/players in Vietnam
   - Both needed for comprehensive coverage

3. BIOGRAPHICAL INDICATORS = GOLD
   - "sinh năm" (born in year) appears in almost ALL person pages
   - "tuổi" (age) is another strong indicator
   - These rarely appear in club/tournament pages

4. TEST BEFORE FULL CRAWL
   - Run test suite first
   - Try depth=3 crawl to verify
   - Adjust weights if needed
